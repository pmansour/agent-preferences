# Cards, programs, and live rules

Start with the private traveler profile, not a fixed list of issuers or loyalty programs. Card benefits depend on the exact product, issuing country, account eligibility, and current terms. Public product pages do not establish what this traveler holds or qualifies for.

## Compare available booking channels

- Compare airline-direct cash, relevant card travel portal cash/points quotes, and reachable airline awards for matched flights.
- Read the actual quoted points and selected card. Check promotional portal rates, legacy eligibility, and cash-plus-points offers without assuming a universal redemption rate.
- Check applicable airfare discounts, companion benefits, credits, and included services. Verify route, cabin, passenger, fare, and booking-channel eligibility. Do not allocate the same credit twice across a trip.
- Use eligible discounted cash fares in the matched comparator. Discounted cash can beat an award that looked attractive against public list prices.
- Distinguish personal/business products and issuing countries. Never apply another variant's rebate or benefit without confirmed eligibility.
- For points rebates, verify qualifying fares, caps, gross points required, and rebate timing. A future rebate cannot fund today's initial debit.
- Include transfer and booking fees. Do not treat incidental credits as automatic airfare rebates.
- Verify fare family, baggage, servicing, earning, and status-credit eligibility for the actual quote. Portal point redemptions and airline awards are different products.

Use official issuer/program terms and relevant live account quotes. Date the evidence. Optional card-linked search tools can aid discovery; verify finalists with the issuing airline and continue direct searches if a tool is unavailable.

## Build a route-specific partner shortlist

Read current transfer menus for the traveler's actual points programs. Verify each candidate booking program can issue the operating carrier and cabin for this route. Include existing airline miles and relevant bilateral partners.

Never infer a direct bank-to-airline transfer because an alliance partner is reachable. Bank transfers, airline partnerships, combining related currencies, household pooling, and ticketing access are separate relationships. Verify each needed relationship and account eligibility. Show the complete cost and delay of intermediate conversions.

## Verify volatile rules

Use official rules and the live quote for booking horizons, release times, pricing, connections, stopovers/open jaws, child/infant tickets, surcharges, account restrictions, transfer timing, holds, and cancellation/redeposit fees. Old skill data and snippets are leads.

A seat map is not award inventory, a waitlist is not a seat, and an operating carrier's member award does not establish partner access. Fare-class meanings vary by airline. Do not turn static sweet-spot charts into bookable inventory.

If rules are inaccessible or ambiguous, mark the affected path conditional and identify what needs confirmation. Continue viable searches. Do not invent transfer speeds, phone numbers, prices, or benefits.
