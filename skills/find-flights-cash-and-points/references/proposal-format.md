# Decision-ready proposals

Lead with a recommendation in 1–3 sentences: exact dates, payment/booking method, whole-party cost and why it wins. State passenger mix, trip constraints and comparison currency once.

## Comparison table

Use 2–5 rows for distinct complete trips or materially different ways to buy them. Do not pad with near-identical transfers. Include cash and points where verified options meet the traveler's stated preferences.

| Proposal | Dates / route / cabin | Whole-party payment | Matched cash / real CPP | Reason / tradeoff |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Best practical cash | Exact outbound/return dates, airports, cabin mix | Comparison-currency total, mandatory extras included | — | Price, elapsed travel, ticket protection |
| Best points option | Exact dates, issuing program, operating flights | Each bank-points and airline-miles balance separately, plus cash | Same-itinerary cash; CPP with currency or N/A | Value, funding, comfort differences |
| Worthwhile alternative | Exact dates and cabins | Full cash/points vector | Matched comparator or explicit gap | Timing, premium cabin, flexibility or access |

For each proposal, add compact segments: flight numbers, operating airline, exact local departure/arrival dates/times, airports, cabin per leg, layover duration/location, and total duration each direction. Flag +1/+2-day arrivals and airport changes. Link observed booking pages; when session URLs are private/unstable, give the canonical site plus reproducible route/date/filter instructions.

Include:

- Passengers actually quoted, sufficient seats verified, timestamp and issuing seller/program. Avoid “last seats” unless explicitly established at that fare.
- Airline miles required versus source-bank transfer, current usable balance, ratio/bonus/rounding and remaining cash. If either of two bank-points programs independently funds the same award, show alternative paths rather than adding them. If funding genuinely combines balances, show each actual debit.
- Material baggage/seating and cancellation/redeposit differences. Identify mixed cabins, especially the long-haul sector.
- Ticket boundaries: protected through-ticket or separate tickets, positioning cost, buffers/overnights and ground segments.
- Practical cash alternative and incremental cost/comfort where it differs from the CPP comparator.

Mark unresolved items on the affected proposal. Quote verification and funding feasibility are distinct. A partial one-way is not a complete-trip proposal.

## Coverage and rejected options

Briefly state date ranges, programs, airline sites and issuer portals checked, when, and material blocked/unsearched areas. Keep the final response short; retain a search notebook for continuation only when useful. If sampled, say “best among dates checked.”

Mention the strongest rejected option when informative: seats, poor value, taxes, long travel, transfer infeasibility, hard-constraint failure. If no complete award qualifies, recommend cash without presenting a chart possibility as bookable.

## Booking handoff

Give concrete steps for the selected option: booking source, exact flights, current points/cash, necessary transfer, verified hold conditions or no-hold timing risk, and deadline. Prepare these before any missing transaction authorization. Do not automatically add credit-card applications, hotels, paid subscriptions or monitoring.

If authorized to execute, recheck current space/price and perform only the authorized actions. Confirm actual ticket issuance and all segments/passengers. Do not embed private identity/account fields in reusable skill files.
